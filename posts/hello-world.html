Hello, world — and why the XMB still matters

2026-07-20 · Saad

The PlayStation 3 dashboard — the XrossMediaBar — is the calmest menu Sony ever shipped. Horizontal categories, vertical items, one silky animated wave in the background. It didn't rush you, and it didn't apologize for taking up the whole screen.

Why remake it as a personal site

A portfolio is a menu. It's a list of things you want the visitor to be able to reach. Most portfolios treat that like a card grid; the XMB treats it like a place. Landing on the home screen should feel like sitting down, not like arriving at a busy intersection.

Interfaces should breathe. Every good menu is mostly empty space.

What lives here

  • Settings — swap between the three themes (Black, Gray, Beige).
  • About — who I am and where else I live online.
  • Blog — this list, plus every post.
  • Projects — things I've built.
  • Friends — links I like.

Everything works with a keyboard, a controller (Xbox, DualShock, or anything the browser exposes as a standard gamepad), a touch screen, and a mouse. No frameworks. No build step. Just three folders.
